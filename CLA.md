## Acceptance

1. Confirm the target repository is dual-licensed (`LICENSE` / SPDX).
2. Accept the **Individual CLA** below via the designated process (CLA bot
   or maintainer-requested PR affirmation) under the GitHub identity that
   authors the commits.
3. If an employer or other entity may own rights in the Contribution, that
   entity must also have a **Corporate CLA** on file before merge.

You agree that Your acceptance of this Agreement — including by CLA-bot
signature, pull-request affirmation, or other process designated by

GitHub account metadata (including username, associated email addresses,

this Agreement.

A Corporate CLA does **not** replace each natural person's Individual CLA
(Apache Software Foundation practice).
